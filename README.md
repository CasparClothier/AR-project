### Audio Restoration Pipeline for Historical Jazz Recordings
A pipeline that diagnoses degradation in historical jazz recordings and accordingly routes through appropriate restoration stages- declipping,
denoising, and bandwidth extension.
## Motivation
Back in the summer of 2023, there was significant activity surrounding Steely Dan's lost media. Their 1980 album Gaucho had an almost mythical set of outtakes and demos-
some of which were in the process of surfacing. As is the case for 40-something year old tape reels that had seen little TLC, they were plagued by clipping, noise and other 
maladies.
